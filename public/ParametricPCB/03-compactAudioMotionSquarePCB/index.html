Turnkey Pre-Assembled Hardware Map

  • MCU: ESP32-C3 SuperMini (Top Layer, offset x=-2.5mm)
  • IMU: MPU-6050 / GY-521 (Backside Layer, x=+9.0mm)
  • Microphones: MAX4466 (Analog G0) & INMP441 (I2S)
  • Tactile Driver: AO3400A N-MOSFET (GPIO 7 PWM)
  • Haptic Protection: SS14 Flyback (D_HAP) + R_GATE 10kΩ
  • High-Side LED Cutoff: AO3401A P-MOS + AO3400A (GPIO 10)
  • Auto Power-Path: AO3401A P-MOS + SS14 + 100kΩ
  • LiPo Charger: MCP73831 + 2kΩ PROG (500mA) + Caps
  • Outputs: 3× Addressable LED Channels & Dual Haptics
  • Power & Controls: 1S LiPo Battery Solder Pads & External Power Switch
  • Mounting: 4× M3 Corner Holes (Ø3.2mm, 31×31mm spacing)
