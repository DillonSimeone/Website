How this was cooked

I started by building out an onion generator. What is the shape of an onion? It's basically a circle or oval with a little pinched top, and then you have roots at the bottom and leafy stalks shooting out of the neck. A bit tedious at first getting all of the parameters keyed in, but once that was done, it was easy to add sliders and a button that added the current JSON settings to the clipboard to paste into the nodejs generator for static .webp images.

Also, I hate having different pages. So I compromised by using iframes in modular-popups. Those iframes are loading seperate pages! Such as red-onion.
