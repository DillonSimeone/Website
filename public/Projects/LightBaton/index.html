Next-Gen Hardware Upgrade: Monolithic Reactive Controller PCBs

Prototype light batons relied on hand-wired perfboards and point-to-point silicone wiring squeezed into 3D-printed core sleds. To elevate the baton into a rugged, stage-ready instrument, Dillon designed and ordered a dedicated production batch of custom 2-layer SMT controller PCBs (JLCPCB fabricated). These boards eliminate rat's-nest wiring, eradicate parasitic battery sleep drain, and introduce dual independent 5V haptic channels.

ESP32-C3 SuperMini MPU6050/6500 IMU Dual 5V Haptics Schottky Flyback Diodes High-Side LED Cut-off TP4056 Power-Path Dual Analog/I2S Mics

Production Gerber Layout: Back-to-back 3D stacked carrier with standard 31×31mm M3 bolt pattern

  • Back-to-Back 3D Component Stacking

    The ESP32-C3 SuperMini MCU mounts on the top surface, while the MPU6050/6500 6-axis IMU mounts directly underneath on the bottom layer. This vertical stacking minimizes I2C trace lengths to under 6mm, isolates high-frequency radio noise, and fits comfortably inside narrow 38mm cylindrical handles.

  • Dual 5V Haptic Channels with Flyback Protection

    Upgraded from a single motor to dual independent vibration channels (Haptic Left & Right). Driven by high-current AO3400A N-channel MOSFETs with 10k gate pull-downs and onboard SS14 Schottky flyback diodes (D_HAP), fully protecting the microcontroller from inductive motor voltage spikes during rapid kinetic swings.

  • Zero-Drain High-Side LED Isolation

    Addressable WS2812B LED pixels continuously consume 1mA to 3mA per pixel even when turned black. A dedicated AO3401A P-channel MOSFET switch (Q_LED_PWR) completely severs 5V rail power to the strip during deep sleep, extending standby battery life from days to months.

  • TP4056 Charging with Auto Power-Path Switching

    Features onboard TP4056 1S LiPo charging via USB-C (R_PROG configured for 500mA fast charge) coupled with an automatic P-FET power-path selector (Q_PWR + D_PWR). Performer batons seamlessly draw continuous power from external USB power banks without cycling or stressing the internal lithium cell.

  • Hybrid Audio + Kinetic Reactive Inputs

    In addition to the 6-axis inertial tracking, the PCB integrates both an analog MAX4466 microphone (ADC1_CH0 / G0) for zero-latency kinetic audio beats and a digital I2S INMP441 microphone (BCLK, WS, SD) for studio-grade FFT frequency band analysis.
