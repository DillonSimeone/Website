FEATURED STAGE HEADLINER

DJwish (Nate Hergert)

Deaf Sonic Agency // Sub-Bass Resonance & Live Synthesis

Portland electronic musician and Deaf advocate Nate Hergert commands a live performance engineered for deep physical chest rumble and sensory immersion. Pairing low-frequency sub-bass modular synthesis with real-time sound-reactive visual telemetry, DJwish redefines electronic music into an intense tactile art form for Deaf and hearing audiences alike.

Live Sub-Bass Telemetry • Real-Time Vest Feed • Stage-to-Chest Vibration
RESEARCH & HARDWARE FRAMEWORK

Universal Music Design (UMD) Tactile Ecosystem

Universal Music Design transforms acoustic sound into a universal language of light, gesture, and bodily tactile vibration. Originating from foundational research by Deaf engineers and researchers, UMD deploys a dedicated 60-unit wearable tactile vest fleet and real-time audio transmission systems to create visceral, shared sensory listening environments.

Lead Design Engineer: Dillon Simeone • Project Director: Shawn Trail, PhD
SPECIAL DEBUT

The Haptic Petting Zoo

Exclusive first public preview of early prototype tactile hardware from Dillon Simeone’s OCF Creative Heights project! Guests are invited to test and explore interactive sensory haptics during dinner and the social hour.

CULINARY EXPERIENCE

Chef Andre Grey

Curated multi-course dinner paired directly with the progression of sound and tactile art. Prepared by CymaSpace Executive Director & Woodstock Cafe Manager Chef Andre (Dre) Grey, with dedicated vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free accommodations.

SENSORY HARDWARE

Wearable Haptic Vests

15–20 individual RF-linked tactile vests translate live sub-bass frequencies directly into physical torso sensation. Experience sound as physical resonance across your chest and back throughout the entire 2-hour dinner.
