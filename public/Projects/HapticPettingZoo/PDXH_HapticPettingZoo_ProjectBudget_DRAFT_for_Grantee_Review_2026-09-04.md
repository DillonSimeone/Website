# The Haptic Petting Zoo
## Project Budget and Disbursement Schedule
*Exhibit B to the Fiscal Sponsorship and Grant Agreement between PDX Hackerspace and Dillon Simeone*

Direct Project costs may total no more than **$91,200.00**, which is the $96,000.00 OCF award less the 5% fiscal sponsorship fee of $4,800.00 retained by Sponsor under Section 2 of the Agreement. The fee is not a Project cost and is not a line in this budget. 

## Project budget, direct costs

| # | Line | Amount |
|---|------|-------:|
| 1 | Artist Fees & Project Direction (Lead Artist & Principal Investigator) | $35,000.00 |
| 2 | Production & Fabricator Stipends (Note 1) | $18,000.00 |
| 3 | Materials, Actuators & Components (Custom PCBs, solenoids, Peltiers, drivers) | $16,500.00 |
| 4 | Tour Equipment & Transit Cases (Ruggedized cases & custom frames) | $6,500.00 |
| 5 | Tour Logistics, Travel & Shipping (2-year Oregon transit, venue support & insurance) (Note 2) | $7,700.00 |
| 6 | Media, Video & ASL Production (High-speed capture & accessible docs) | $4,500.00 |
| 7 | Hardware Maintenance Reserve (Field repairs & wear replacement stock) | $3,000.00 |
|   | **Total direct Project costs (cap under the Agreement: $91,200.00)** | **$91,200.00** |

**Note 1, fabricator stipends.** This line covers stipends for fabricators, technicians, and accessibility specialists Grantee selects on skill, whether or not they are members of PDX Hackerspace, with the line able to cover a paid PDX Hackerspace membership for any collaborator who needs one to work on the Project at the space. Grantee engages and pays collaborators under Section 11 of the Agreement; no director or officer of PDX Hackerspace, and no family member of one, may be paid from the Grant without prior approval of Sponsor's disinterested directors.

**Note 2, insurance.** Liability insurance for the public exhibition and tour is charged to this line under Section 15 of the Agreement.
