*Prepared specifically for Dillon Simeone regarding sole proprietorship taxation, cash-basis accounting, and year-end tax liability.*

### 1. How Grant Funds Are Taxed for a Sole Proprietor
Because you are signing as a Sole Proprietor with your SSN, PDX Hackerspace will report total disbursements made to you on **IRS Form 1099-NEC (Nonemployee Compensation)**.
* On your federal tax return, you report this income on **Schedule C (Form 1040: Profit or Loss From Business)**.
* As a sole proprietor, you operate on a **cash-basis accounting system**.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Taxable Net Profit} = \text{Total Cash Received in 2026} - \text{Allowable Deductible Expenses Paid in 2026}$$
