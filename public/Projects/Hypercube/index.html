The Tedious Art of 5-Panel Cube Soldering

Every maker who has tackled an LED cube knows: the true challenge isn't the software, it's the 15+ delicate wire bridges along internal 90-degree corner seams. Each panel requires 5V, Ground, and Data-Out-to-Data-In routing inside a cramped 3D cube interior. Without precision soldering and multi-point power injection, the fifth panel dims or browns out. Each Hypercube commissioned from Dillon is hand-aligned, heat-tested for thermal stability, and built to last.
