BLE Input Switcher

A dedicated hardware input switcher engineered around the dual-core Raspberry Pi Pico 2W (RP2350) and a WeAct 2.9" tri-color (Black/White/Red) SPI E-Ink display (SSD1680).

The Pico advertises over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.2) and listens for switching commands from a companion Flutter Android mobile app. When an input is triggered, the firmware arbitrates GPIO output lines with mutual exclusivity and refreshes the partial-update E-Ink panel to show the active channel status with zero standby power draw.
