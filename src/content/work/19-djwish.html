DJ Deaf Wish: Sound-Reactive Stage Suite & Universal Music Design

An immersive Three.js WebXR live performance suite and street-art portal dedicated to Portland-based Deaf musician and advocate Nate Hergert (DJ Deaf Wish). Transmutes the archival golden pyramid and laser artwork into an interactive 3D universe—featuring a stepped metallic pyramid with sub-bass pulsing apex, multi-colored additive laser fan projecting the "DJ DEAF WISH" neon logo into starry deep space, and cinematic camera choreography.

Engineered with a real-time Web Audio API synthesizer for headphone and desktop previews, WebXR immersive VR session support, and reactive stage lights tuned to 20Hz–160Hz low-end transients.
