Gaussian Splat Studio (xRweb Experience)

An interactive WebGL xRweb experience utilizing 3D Gaussian Splatting rendered via Three.js. Captured with Postshot and exported into high-density PLY point splat radiance fields, this project brings photorealistic 3D volumetric environment reconstruction directly to modern browsers.
