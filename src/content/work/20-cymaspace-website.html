CymaSpace: New Website Design & Community Hub

I designed the front page for CymaSpace's new website, creating a clean, responsive, and welcoming digital entryway for the Portland-based, Deaf-owned arts and technology nonprofit. CymaSpace works at the forefront of inclusive culture—making sound, music, and performance tangible through cymatics, dynamic LED visuals, haptics, and assistive technology.

The redesigned homepage brings CymaSpace's multifaceted initiatives together into a cohesive, accessible experience. It highlights active community programming, sign language education, and direct pathways to their core project archive—including the Universal Music Design initiative, Sign Kids, and community events at the Woodstock Cafe.
