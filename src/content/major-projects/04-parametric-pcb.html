Parametric PCB/Modeling Suite

I used to design 3D models and circuit boards entirely by hand, but building them with code and parameters feels like a return to the original ancient magic, where math, logic, and structure define form. This suite pairs parametric 3D modeling with a turnkey circuit board generator engineered with tscircuit, TypeScript, and Three.js—featuring interactive 2D/3D board inspection, automated JLCPCB SMT manufacturing DRC checks, real-time BOM/CPL generation, and production exports for audio/motion-reactive LED batons and haptic controllers.
