3D Model Racing & Grass Study

3D Model Racing is a browser-based 3D racing game engineered in Three.js (ESM). It allows players to import custom 3D models (STL, OBJ, GLB) or race formula prototypes across procedural ribbon tracks with rolling hills, multi-lane AI competitors, dynamic day-night atmospheric lighting, drifting physics, and synthesized Web Audio sound effects.

To power the expansive outdoor terrain, the Grass Study Lab serves as a dedicated real-time meadow shader testbed. It implements high-performance instanced GLSL triangle grass (Peter Adams Ghibli technique) featuring dynamic wind turbulence, wildflower distribution, and real-time interactive vehicle trample physics where blades flatten under tires.
