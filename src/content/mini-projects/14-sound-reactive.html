Sound-Reactive Arts

A high-performance workspace showcasing audio-reactive WebGL and Three.js visual installations. Features a real-time shader-warped Sonic Sphere and dynamic raymarching Sound Tunnels that weave, spin, and curve to the rhythm of sound.

It maps real-time frequency data (0-20kHz) to geometry, motion, and color, creating vibrant, pulsating 3D visualizations that react to every beat.
