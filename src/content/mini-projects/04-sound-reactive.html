Sound-Reactive Arts // Deaf DJ Suite

A real-time WebGL and Three.js visual installation suite tailored for deaf DJs and live projection mapping. Features 10 sound-reactive shaders driven by full 20Hz (Crimson Red) to 20,000Hz (Violet/Air) FFT spectrum decomposition, tactile beat-sync metronomes, transient flash indicators, and interactive 4-point corner pinning for angled stage facades.
