The Backrooms: Deconstructive Roguelite

An infinite 3D first-person procedural survival roguelite set across canonical Backrooms strata, built with modern Three.js (ESM) and Web Audio API synthesis. In this reality, the liminal labyrinth is not a static maze—it is a completely harvestable, destructible resource grid.

Demolish damp monosodium yellow wallpaper, fluorescent luminaires, dropped acoustic ceiling tiles, and structural subfloors to extract drywall plaster, copper wiring, ballasts, and scrap. Breach through vertical floor shafts and ceilings to descend between stratified realms—from the endless office corridors of Level 0 to the raw concrete pipe corridors of Level 1, the serene turquoise waters of Level 37 (The Poolrooms), and the subterranean vaults of Level -2. Features an improvised crafting workbench, modular scaffolding towers, drywall barricades, reality-bending perks, and autonomous AI salvage mining rovers that drill nearby structures and return with salvage.
