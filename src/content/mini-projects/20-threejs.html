ThreeJs & WebGL 3D Interactive Lab

A showcase of WebGL experiments exploring persistent 3D worlds, procedural physics simulations, spatial computing, and webcam volumetric reconstruction powered by Three.js.

3D Model Racing & Grass Study Lab: A browser-based racing engine in Three.js (ESM) supporting custom 3D model imports (STL, OBJ, GLB), procedural ribbon tracks with rolling hills, AI competitors, day/night atmospheric lighting, and drifting physics. Powered by an instanced GLSL triangle meadow grass shader (Peter Adams Ghibli technique) featuring dynamic wind turbulence and real-time wheel trample physics.

  • Play 3D Model Racing
  • Launch Grass Study Lab

The Backrooms: Deconstructive Roguelite: An infinite 3D first-person procedural survival game set across canonical Backrooms strata, built with modern Three.js (ESM) and Web Audio API synthesis. In this reality, the liminal labyrinth is an entirely harvestable, destructible resource grid.

  • Play The Backrooms Roguelite

Flesh-Architect (Reach for the Breathing Stars): An interactive 3D physics sculpture and creature simulation combining Three.js and Cannon-es rigid body dynamics. Explores organic kinetic geometry, reactive tension, and tactile procedural entity choreography directly in WebGL.

  • Launch 3D Physics Simulation

Hands to STL: Captures hand poses from a webcam in real-time via MediaPipe and converts them directly into 3D printable STL files in Three.js. Supports solid bone structures, winding ribbons, crystalline formations, and organic metaballs.

  • Launch Full Hands to STL Studio
  • Low-Poly Mesh Generator
  • Mesh Surface Generator

3D Gaussian Splat Rotary Scanner: Real-time low-resolution Gaussian splats generated from MediaPipe face and hand landmarking. Splat point clouds are positioned around a virtual 3D turntable in 3-second capture loops, dissolving back into powder on tap or Spacebar hit.
