Firmware Labs & Robotics Workbench

  • MIDI LED Brace: Wearable stage performance armpiece synced over MIDI clock and note triggers to animate high-density WS2812B strips in real time.

  • NeoPixel Fuel Rods: Diffused cylindrical reactor core lighting running smooth organic palette blending algorithms on ESP32-C3.

  • RatVehicle Rover & Drone Wheels: Dual-motor wheeled robotic rovers and video-streaming chassis testbeds with autonomous steering algorithms.

  • mmWave Radar Human Detector: Hardware presence sensing module triggering room lighting and haptic alerts without relying on optical cameras.

  • Visual Notification Clocks: Silent visual timekeepers (DeafClock and custom PCBClock) replacing acoustic alarms with pulsed chromatic rings.
