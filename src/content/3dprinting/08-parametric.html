Parametric Modeling Library & Ultrasonic Haptics

Parametric models are designs defined by math and code, allowing you to change dimensions on the fly without touching a CAD program. Unlike Makerlab, which processes everything on their servers, this entire stack runs client-side directly in your browser. It's faster, private, and handles complex geometric transitions like my airflow adapters and acoustic lenses with zero lag!

A prime application is the Ultrasonic Mid-Air Haptic Dish: a parametrically generated acoustic focus bowl designed to concentrate 40kHz airborne ultrasound waves onto skin nerve endings without physical touch.
