"I Love You" ASL Hand

Price: $20.00

A solid 3D-printed ambient sculpture with no moving parts; just warm, clean light radiating gently from within. It forms the iconic "I Love You" (ILY) sign in American Sign Language. As a Deaf engineer and maker, this piece is deeply personal to me: a celebration of Deaf culture, visual communication, and the expressive beauty of sign language crafted right in my studio.

Programmed with a gentle, slow-shifting color cycle, each solid hand provides a soothing ambient glow that transitions through a curated spectrum. A beautiful desktop companion for makers, Deaf and hard-of-hearing community members, ASL learners, and anyone who appreciates the power of visual language.
