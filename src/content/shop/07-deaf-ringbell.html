Deaf Ringbell Detector (Acoustic ESP-NOW Alert System)

Price: $140.00 (Starter Kit: 1 Leader + 1 Follower) • $75.00 (Leader) • $65.00 (Follower)

A non-invasive digital bridge for visual accessibility in the home. Designed from personal necessity as a Deaf engineer, this system acoustically detects existing mechanical or electronic door chimes and broadcasts instant, zero-latency strobe alerts across rooms via wireless ESP-NOW broadcast.

100% Local & Private: This system is completely standalone. It communicates peer-to-peer directly between ESP32 microcontrollers over low-latency radio packets; no Wi-Fi credentials to configure, no cloud servers, no phone apps, no monthly subscription fees, and zero internet dependencies. It keeps running even if your home Wi-Fi or broadband is down.

How It Works: The Leader Node (Ears) mounts cleanly next to your doorbell chime and uses an I2S digital microphone calibrated with Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) signal processing to trigger only on the chime's acoustic frequency, ignoring conversation, barking dogs, and TV noise. The Follower Node (Eyes) features a high-intensity 8x8 WS2812B matrix that strobes vividly upon trigger and maintains a gentle status heartbeat when idle.

Built-in Captive Portal: Connect to the device's temporary setup access point at 192.168.4.1 to view live 60-second acoustic waveform graphs, trigger audit logs, and tune microphone sensitivity to your specific chime.
