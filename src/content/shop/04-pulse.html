PULSE: Haptic Ecosystem

Starting at $65.00 / $140.00 Starter Pair

The PULSE ecosystem is a professional-grade hardware and software framework for high-fidelity tactile feedback. This system uses an ESP32-based "Leader" unit to capture real-time audio via an I2S digital microphone, perform on-chip FFT frequency analysis, and broadcast synchronized commands to multiple "Follower" haptic saucers via ESP-NOW with sub-10ms latency.

The Follower units feature a rugged, parametric "sled-in-shell" mechanical design and a refined tactile response, ideal for live music performance, gaming immersion, sensory rooms, or accessibility research.

Pure Kinetic Force (Safe for Pacemakers & Cochlear Implants): Commercial haptic vests are heavy, hot, and rely on massive voice-coil magnets that emit intense electromagnetic fields; making them unsafe for people with cardiac pacemakers, ICDs, or sensitive cochlear implants. PULSE was born out of frustration with those vests. It delivers pure, direct kinetic force without heavy voice-coil magnets, generating zero dangerous magnetic radiation. Lightweight and modular, you can slip a puck into a pocket, tuck it against clothing, or set it directly on an instrument or tabletop.

Note: It's hard to take a good picture of haptics in action! The rugged circular saucer casing protects the internal motor and electronics from physical impacts and water during high-energy live performance.
