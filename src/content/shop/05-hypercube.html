Sound-Reactive LED Hypercube

Price: $150.00

Basically every maker has built an LED cube, but this one is engineered with organic, sound-reactive 3D ripples. Five 8x8 WS2812B matrix panels (320 individually addressable LEDs total) wrap seamlessly around a custom 3D printed chassis beneath a textured semi-translucent diffusion hood.

An onboard ESP32-C3 microcontroller samples ambient audio through an adjustable-gain MAX4466 analog microphone, translating acoustic vibrations into radial color ripples: deep crimson red for low sub-bass, shifting forward through amber, green, cyan, and vivid purple as frequencies climb.

Universal USB-C Power: Powered directly from any standard USB-C phone charger, laptop port, or portable 5V power bank, just like your smartphone (USB-C power cable included). Plug it in at your desk, studio shelf, or take it on the go with a battery pack.

Active Ripple State: Frequency-reactive purple/pink illumination through textured hood
Mechanical Form Factor: 5-face 3D printed diffusion cube and weighted electronics stand
