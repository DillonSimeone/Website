GeLu Bracers (Research Grade)

Starting at $110.00 (Single) / $200.00 (Custom-Tailored Bilateral Pair)

The GeLu (GestoLumina) Bracers are a wearable haptic interface born out of research dedicated to the Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing community. This is a refined version of the hardware used in my research into universal music design and environmental awareness.

These bracers have been tuned to such high fidelity that they allow the wearer to distinguish between different musical instruments through tactile feedback. It's not just a gadget; it's a sensory expansion tool.

Because capturing tactile vibrations on video is challenging, the bracer incorporates precision LED illumination that matches the exact vibration harmonics in real time. This breakthrough led directly to my first published research paper!

Custom-Tailored Fit: While originally modeled around my own wrist, each commission is custom-fitted. Simply send your wrist and forearm circumference measurements, and I will parametrically adjust the 3D-printed enclosure curvature and strap anchors specifically for your body.
