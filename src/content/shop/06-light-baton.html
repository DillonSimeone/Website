Motion-Reactive Haptic Light Baton

Price: $200.00

Swing it, it vibrates and glows! The Motion-Reactive Light Baton is an expressive kinetic performance instrument integrating 6-axis motion tracking, high-torque haptic vibration feedback, and dense addressable LED diffusion. As you swing, spin, and strike, internal inertial sensors map your kinetic velocity into real-time tactile pulses and trailing chromatic wave sweeps.

Universal Parametric Core: Powered by Dillon's custom 3D parametric CAD engine, this baton is not locked to one rigid shell. It can be parametrically adapted and 3D printed around virtually any structural core; whether you prefer a flexible bamboo shaft, rigid steel rod, carbon fiber tube, or a natural tree branch.

Wireless ESP-NOW Broadcast: The baton doesn't just illuminate itself; its onboard ESP32-C3 broadcasts real-time kinetic telemetry over wireless ESP-NOW with sub-10ms latency, letting you remotely control stage lighting, room fixtures, and wearable accessories in sync with your physical movements.

Incoming Hardware Evolution: Dillon has designed and ordered a production run of custom monolithic 38×38mm SMT controller PCBs featuring 3D back-to-back component stacking, dual independent 5V haptic drivers, high-side zero-drain LED cut-offs, and auto power-path LiPo charging. All upcoming commissions will be built with these upgraded boards!
