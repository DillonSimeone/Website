            COMMISSION PROCESS

            How Hardware Commissions Work

            Just like commissioning an artist for a bespoke painting, every piece here is custom-fabricated, hand-soldered, programmed, and calibrated in my lab. You can commission any project below, or request custom adaptations to suit your exact space. Nothing falls through the cracks:

            1

            Click "Commission this project!"

            Pick any piece below and hit the commission button. It will open your email client (or Gmail) with a pre-filled template containing project details, unit options, and setup questions ready to send.

            2

            Consultation & Details

            I will reply with follow-up questions to confirm your specific requirements: custom enclosure colors, mounting setup, cable lengths, or custom firmware behaviors.

            3

            Square Invoice

            Once we finalize the specs and timeline, I will send an official, secure invoice through Square. Payment secures your build slot on the lab workbench.

            4

            Bench Build & Delivery

            Your hardware is 3D printed, soldered, programmed, and thoroughly tested before delivery. Shipped via PirateShip with tracked USPS delivery (with in-person setup and acoustic tuning available for local Oregon residents).
