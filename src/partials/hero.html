Welcome! I’m a maker, developer, and Lead Design Engineer with over a decade of experience turning ideas into working physical things: custom stage hardware, interactive installations, accessible tools, and rapid prototypes. You can explore some of my builds on YouTube.

Commission me like an artist, for hardware: Just as someone commissions an illustrator for a custom painting, you can commission me for bespoke physical hardware. Whether you need a non-invasive visual alert device (like the Deaf Ringbell Detector), custom wearable haptics, motion-reactive ambient lighting, or a one-of-a-kind embedded prototype, I design, solder, 3D print, and calibrate hardware to order in my lab. Invoiced securely via Square, with local Oregon installation available.

As a Deaf technologist and interface designer, sensory agency is the foundation of everything I build. My ongoing work on the Haptic Petting Zoo was recently honored with a $96,000 Creative Heights award from the Oregon Community Foundation, translating acoustic frequencies and spatial resonance directly into tactile vibrations felt through skin and bone.
