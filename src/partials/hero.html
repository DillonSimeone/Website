Dillon Simeone

A multifaceted innovator driving accessibility in technology.

Welcome! I’m a maker, developer, and Lead Design Engineer with over a decade of experience turning ideas into working things: responsive web applications, custom stage hardware, interactive installations, and rapid prototypes. While engineering is my day job, I love taking on creative challenges of all shapes and sizes, from 3D printed mechanisms to novel gadgets and accessible tools. You can explore some of my builds on my YouTube!

As a Deaf technologist and interface designer, inclusivity and sensory agency are the foundation of everything I build. My current ongoing project is the Haptic Petting Zoo, recently honored with a $96,000 Creative Heights award from the Oregon Community Foundation for a two-year touring exhibition across Oregon, fiscally sponsored by Control+H Hackerspace.

Why is this work so impactful? Traditional media has always treated sound as an auditory-only privilege. The Haptic Zoo fundamentally reimagines that relationship by translating acoustic frequencies, musical harmonics, and spatial resonances directly into physical vibrations felt through skin and bone. It turns sound into an open, tangible sensory playground that unites Deaf and hearing communities, proving that tactile accessibility is not an afterthought, but an expressive creative medium.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me outdoors, gaming, or in the workshop tinkering with laser-cut designs. I’m constantly chasing new skills and challenges, with a steady focus on making technology more accessible and engaging for everyone.

If it can be built, I’ll find a way to make it happen! Let’s talk, whether it’s an accessibility tool, a custom gadget, or an art piece that needs a little engineering magic.
