All-SMD Integrated Hardware

  • MCU Core: ESP32-C3-WROOM-02-N4 (Castellated SMD, Native USB CDC/JTAG)
  • Motion Sensor: MPU-6050 6-Axis Gyro/Accelerometer (QFN-24, I2C)
  • Audio Sensor: INMP441 Omnidirectional Digital I2S MEMS Mic (SMD-9)
  • Power System: USB-C 16P + 1S LiPo Charger (MCP73831) + Auto Power-Path
  • Voltage Rail: ME6211C33M5G-N 3.3V 500mA Low Dropout LDO
  • LED Output: Switched 5V/VSYS Header with High-Side P-MOSFET Isolator
  • Haptic Driver: AO3400A N-MOSFET PWM + SS14 Flyback Diode
  • Diagnostic: 5-Pin MPU I2C Tap Header, Hardware Reset & Boot Buttons
  • Mounting: 4× M3 Corner Holes (Ø3.2mm Drill, Ø4.8mm Pad)
  • Assembly: 100% Single-Order Factory SMT by JLCPCB (Zero Hand Soldering)
