💡 Signal Current Note

Pins PD2, PD3, and PC3 are logic-level control lines only. They trigger external power drivers and must never directly drive motor or Peltier currents.

⚠️ Programming & Power Conflict

Never connect the WCH-LinkE target-power pin (3V3/5V) while the CH32V003 board is plugged into USB-C. Connect only SWIO (PD1) and GND when using USB power!
