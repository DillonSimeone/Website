Kit Description & Notes

The DFRobot FireBeetle ESP32-C6 Mini was specifically selected for this workshop kit because it features an onboard LiPo charger circuit.

Each kit includes 5 custom weighted haptic heads designed to fit directly over the brass adapter that is superglued to the motor shafts.

⚡ Recharging Note: To recharge when a LiPo battery is attached, toggle the power switch to ON, then plug in the USB-C cable.
