Turnkey Pre-Assembled Hardware Map

  • MCU: ESP32-C3 SuperMini (Dual THT + SMD)
  • IMU: MPU-6050 / MPU-6500 (8-Pin Dual Mount)
  • Microphones: MAX4466 (Analog) & INMP441 (I2S)
  • Tactile Driver: AO3400A N-MOSFET (GPIO 7 PWM)
  • Haptic Protection: D_HAP B5819W Flyback + R_GATE 10kΩ
  • Auto Power-Path: AO3401A P-MOS + B5819W + 100kΩ
  • LiPo Charger: TP4054 + 2kΩ PROG (500mA) + Caps
  • Outputs: Dual Haptic (L/R), 3× LED Ports (High-Side Cutoff) & Mode Button
  • Power Control: 2-Pin External Switch Header (THT)
  • Mounting: 4× M3 Corner Holes (Ø3.2mm)
