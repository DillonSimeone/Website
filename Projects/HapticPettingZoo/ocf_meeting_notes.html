Q1 How is the project going so far? Has anything shifted?
Foundation & Prototyping
"The first check cleared through Control+H. We're assembling the benchtop proof-of-concept prototype for mid-November so people can physically interact with it before joining the team."
  • The Paradigm Shift: Each time people hear about haptics, they default to "assistive speech-to-text wearables" for hearing convenience. No! Haptics is an expressive fine-art medium like oil painting or synth sound design. Having a physical demo destroys that misconception immediately.
  • Pacing Strategy (The Shift): When this grant came through, it blew up my original year in the best way possible! I deliberately set Q4 2026 as low-burn prototyping while wrapping up my dual AS/BS degree requirements.
  • Full Throttle in 2027: Heavy fabrication, bulk actuator purchases, and contractor stipends begin in January 2027 (Milestone 2) when I have the 100% bandwidth this project deserves.
  • Active Momentum Drop: Tomorrow I'm staging a forest performance for a Deaf DJ with wireless sound-reactive lights and haptics—proving the sensory translation pipelines in real-time before full zoo builds.

Q2 What do you want to get out of this journey or experience?
Leadership & Growth
"As a Deaf engineer who has worked in isolation or with Deaf teams, this is my first time leading a multidisciplinary team across language barriers—sprinting after a rolling ball instead of pushing it uphill alone."
  • Inclusive Technical Leadership: Using modern tech (live speech-to-text, video relay, shared CAD schematics) to eliminate friction and direct skilled hearing specialists (soft-goods, machinists, textile designers).
  • Advanced Fabrication Access: Partnering with Control+H gives us industrial-grade tooling (like precision fiber laser cutting) to turn scrap & structural metals into rugged interactive enclosures and tactile skins.
  • Legitimizing Tactile Arts: Showing that touch is not just 'phone buzzes'—it encompasses thermal sensations (Peltier coolers), kinetic displacement, air pressure, and resonant acoustic tactile feedback.

✓ Golden Framing

"Partnering with Control+H gives us direct access to precision tooling (like fiber laser cutting) for custom metallic tactile skins."
