# Apex Parasite: Chimera Odyssey — Complete System Codex & Architectural Design

## 1. High Concept & The Great Awakening

You are not the chosen one. You are not a solitary protagonist.
You are simply **The Worm** (`@`) — one of many ancient cosmic biological parasites stirring from eons of slumber in an earth-bound dark fantasy world where human kingdoms and wild elemental beasts clash.


* **Human Champions & War-Beast Packs (The Hunters)**:
  * As you grow more monstrous and commit atrocities, it is not other horrors that pursue you—other cosmic entities do not care.
  * Instead, **Human Champions and elite Monster Trainers** form specialized hunting inquisitions with coordinated packs of trained war-beasts (a grim, lethal distortion of Pokémon trainers).
  * High-tier champions possess martial techniques, alchemical fire, and cyber-relics specifically tuned to purge biological abominations.
* **Depravity & Aberrant Tumors**:
  * Unbridled slaughter and live vivisections increase global Depravity.
  * Higher Depravity mutates the biosphere: wildlife develops aberrant tumors, extra weeping ocular clusters, and hyper-aggressive fungal growths—subtle, horrifying hints of the cosmic nightmare waking beneath the crust.

## 2. Equipment, Clothing, Wrappings & Mental Bandwidth

Body parts do not exist in a vacuum. Your chimera can scavenge, wear, and wield manufactured items:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  EQUIPMENT & WEAPON CLASSIFICATION                                           │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  CATEGORY       │ BANDWIDTH DRAW │ CONCEALMENT │ SPECIAL TRAITS              │
├─────────────────┼────────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ Instinctive Claw│ 0 Bandwidth    │ -10%        │ Organic tendon reflex.      │

│ Human Firearm   │ 5 Bandwidth    │ +5% (Cased) │ Recoil, aim tracking, ammo. │
│ Linen Bandages  │ 0 Bandwidth    │ +10%        │ Stops bleeding, hides rot.  │
│ Chainlink Wrap  │ 0 Bandwidth    │ -5% (Heavy) │ +Armor, +Weight (Cal burn). │

│ Leather Rucksack│ 0 Bandwidth    │ Neutral     │ Expands cargo space (+8).   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### A. Mental Bandwidth System
* Operating tools requires neural concentration:
  * **Biological Limbs (Claws, Stingers)**: Run on pure motor instinct (**0 to 1 Bandwidth**).
  * **Simple Handheld Melee (Broadsword, Mace)**: Requires moderate coordination (**2 Bandwidth**).
  * **Firearms & Ranged Mechanisms (Revolver, Musket)**: Demands heavy focus to aim, manage recoil, and track sights (**5 Bandwidth**).
* If total Bandwidth exceeds your **Neural Capacity**, your chimera experiences accuracy tremors, delayed reactions, and muscle spasms.

### B. Wrappings, Armor & Bandages
* **Linen Bandages**: Can be wrapped around practically any appendage. Accelerates clotting, prevents wound sepsis, and covers raw exposed meat to preserve your disguise.
* **Chainlink Wrappings & Wire Reinforcements**: Heavy metallic mesh wrapped around forearms and torsos. Provides substantial physical armor against slicing strikes, but increases structural weight and calorie burn per movement.
* **Treated Beast Hides & Chitin Sheets**: Rough organic plates strapped over joints to grant elemental insulation (Fire/Frost resistance).

### C. Concealing Fabrics & Cargo
* **Traveler's Cloak / Burlap Cowl**: Heavy flowing garments designed to conceal abnormal silhouettes. Drapes over extra arms, grafted torsos, and neck tumors to maintain your **Concealment Rating**.
* **Backpacks & Storage Glands**: Human leather knapsacks or grown internal pouches strapped to dorsal plates to increase organ carrying capacity.

## 3. Fast Turn-Based Combat & Asymmetric Resilience

### A. Fluid Turn-Based Rhythm & Visual Hover Reticle
* Time advances seamlessly with player input (the game moves fast as you act).
* **Hover Reticle**:
  * Hovering the cursor over any enemy body part instantly pops an intuitive tactical reticle displaying:
    `[ 🎯 Forearm: 78% Hit Chance | 18-24 Expected Slicing Dmg | Armor: 4 Deflection ]`
  * Clicking strikes that limb immediately!

```
[ TARGET ENEMY ]                                    [ PLAYER WORM CHIMERA ]
- Head / Sensory Nexus                              - Branching Limbs / Torsos
- Thorax / Mantle Core                              - Host Flesh Cage
- Appendages (Claws, Wings, Legs)                   - NESTED WORM CORE (@)
* Destroy Vital (Brain/Heart) = INSTANT DEATH!      * Head Decapitated? KEEPS FIGHTING!
                                                    * Heart Stopped? KEEPS FIGHTING!
                                                    * Only dies if WORM CORE is breached!
```

* **Asymmetric Worm Resilience**:
  * Baseline humans and beasts die when their head or heart is obliterated.
  * For you, **The Worm (`@`) is the true organism**. A human host with a worm in it can be decapitated in battle, and the headless body **continues fighting relentlessly**! It only perishes if the Worm's specific nesting node itself is pierced or completely drained of calories and blood.
* **Vital Targeting Difficulty**:
  * Targeting vital organs (Brain / Heart) suffers heavy miss penalties (`-40% Hit Chance`, active enemy dodging). Breaking through skulls requires staggering, webbing, or pinning the target first.

### B. Blood Tracking, Expanding Pools & Hemorrhagic Collapse
* Slicing wounds cause arterial hemorrhage (`-X Blood/round`).
* **Expanding Blood Pool**: An interactive dark crimson pool dynamically grows on the floor beneath combatants during battle.
* **Hemorrhagic Collapse (Incapacitation)**:
  * When blood volume drops below 20% or pain shock peaks, the enemy collapses unconscious into the blood pool.
  * Unconscious enemies can be bound in webbing or sedatives, transitioning into a **Living Specimen**.

## 4. The Live Specimen Surgical Mini-Game

When an encounter concludes, the camera fades, pulls back, sweeps across the scene, tilts, and **zooms smoothly down toward the body**:
* **Dead Corpse**: Shadowy figure sprawled still in the blood pool.
* **Living Specimen**: Shadowy figure wriggling and groaning in the blood pool.

```
                     LIVING SPECIMEN SURGICAL FIELD
                 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
                 │ SPECIMEN VITAL PULSE: 72% [WEAK]  │
                 │ Drains on incision | Flatline = 0%│
                 └─────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                   │
         ┌─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                   ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐                     ┌───────────────────────────┐
│ 🦞 Serrated Scythe Forearm│                     │ 🧠 Intact Sensory Brain   │
│ Pristine • Tier: Rare     │                     │ Pristine • Tier: Epic     │
│ Shock Cost: -10% Pulse    │                     │ Shock Cost: -40% Pulse    │
│ [EXTRACT COMPONENT]       │                     │ [EXTRACT COMPONENT]       │
└───────────────────────────┘                     └───────────────────────────┘
```

### The Surgical Triage Loop
1. **The Vital Pulse Meter**:
   - The living specimen has a **Vital Pulse / Heartbeat meter** (e.g. 70%).
   - Every cut, nerve clamp, or organ extraction drains Vital Pulse due to surgical shock and blood loss.
2. **Skillful Extraction**:
   - If you are smart in your surgery order (clamping arteries, harvesting peripheral limbs first, extracting delicate glands right before the heart gives out), you can harvest multiple pristine components from a single specimen!
3. **Systemic Flatline Collapse**:
   - If the specimen flatlines and dies mid-surgery: **Systemic necrosis triggers!** Blood coagulates instantly, delicate gland membranes rupture, and all remaining unextracted organs suffer heavy degradation or become completely unharvestable.

## 5. Balanced Alchemy Synthesis (Labyrinth of Refrain Model)

Grafting unwanted equipment and biological fodder into a primary base organ:
* **1 Base Organ**: The piece you want to keep and reinforce (e.g. your favorite Epic Scythe).
* **Up to 8 Fodder Organs**: Unwanted limbs, captured beast appendages, and wild drops consumed in the crucible.
* **Balance Controls (Preventing Overpowered Snowballing)**:
  * **Reinforcement Limit**: Items have an absorption cap based on rarity (Common: 3 syntheses max, Rare: 6, Legendary: 10).
  * **Diminishing Returns**: Fodder stat inheritance tapers off (1st fodder adds 20% of its stats, 8th adds 5%).
  * **Bio-Slurry / Mana Costs**: Synthesis requires melting down biomass into bio-slurry; deep-game upgrades demand substantial reserves.
  * **Calorie & Bandwidth Burden**: Heavily augmented weapons naturally demand slightly more metabolic calories and concentration to swing.

## 6. Organ Maturation & DCSS Cross-Training

* **Limb XP**: Attached parts level up with use (Ranks 1 to 10).
  * Increases base damage and critical chance.
  * **Reduces Calorie & Bandwidth Draw by ~5% per rank** (at Rank 10, the nervous system carries the limb with ~50% peak metabolic efficiency; it never zeros out completely).
* **DCSS Cross-Training Webs**:
  * Training **Slashing Limbs** (Scythes, Claws) feeds **40% free Cross-XP** into **Piercing Limbs** (Stingers, Fangs) and vice versa.
  * Training **Crushing Limbs** cross-trains **Carapaces & Shields**.

## 7. Step-by-Step Gameplay Walkthrough: Start to Forest Combat

This scenario illustrates what the player experiences, how the Persona 5–styled layered menus look, and how the player interacts with the game:

### Step 1: Start Menu (The Awakening)
* **Visuals**:
  * Deep black screen with glowing cyan and crimson nerve-fiber scanlines.
  * In the center, a 2.5D stylized silhouette of **The Worm (`@`)** pulses with rhythmic heartbeat audio (`BioAudio`).
  * Bold, angular, Persona 5–style slash typography:
    - `[ INITIATE HOST INFESTATION ]`
    - `[ BIO-ESSENCE SANCTUARY (META) ]`
    - `[ CODEX & MUTATION RECORDS ]`
* **Interaction**:
  * Hovering an option triggers an electric nerve twitch sound and a sharp chromatic camera tilt.
  * Clicking `[ INITIATE HOST INFESTATION ]` triggers a sudden cinematic screen shatter as the Worm burrows into a baseline humanoid host vessel.

### Step 2: The Frontier City (The Concealment Hub)
* **Visuals**:
  * High-contrast 2.5D parallax backdrop: Rain-slick cobblestones, timbered medieval storefronts, distant cathedral spires, and flickering gas lanterns.
  * The scene drifts smoothly in reaction to mouse movement.
* **Top Status Strip**:
  * `VESSEL: Human Vagrant` • `MASS: 4 Parts` • `CALORIES: 110/120` • `CONCEALMENT: 90% [HUMANOID DISGUISE]` • `DEPRAVITY: 0`
* **Action Menu (Angular Persona 5 Slash Ribbon)**:
  * `[ 🛒 WANDER COBBLESTONE MARKET ]` (Concealment $\ge$ 50%): Buy bandages, hunting knives, linen cloaks.
  * `[ 🍺 ENTER HUNTER TAVERN ]`: Eavesdrop on rumors of a rare Verdant Thorn-Beast spotted in the eastern canopy.
  * `[ 🌲 VENTURE INTO THE PRIMEVAL FOREST ]`: Leave the city gates and begin the wilderness trek.
  * `[ 🩸 INCITE MIDNIGHT SLAUGHTER ]` (Highlighted in crimson): Raid the city garrison (warns: destroys disguise, raises alarm).
* **Action**:
  * The player clicks `[ 🌲 VENTURE INTO THE PRIMEVAL FOREST ]`. Calorie burn ticks down by 4 units as the travel occurs.

### Step 3: The Primeval Forest (Exploration & Parallax Staging)
* **Visuals**:
  * The screen sweeps into a lush, moody emerald canopy.
  * **Parallax Layers Active**:
    - *Layer 0 (Back)*: Pale dawn sky filtered through mist.
    - *Layer 1 (Mid-Back)*: Silhouetted giant redwoods and ancient mountain crags.
    - *Layer 2 (Midground)*: Gnarled mossy trees, hanging vines, falling green leaves.
    - *Layer 3 (Atmosphere)*: Procedural volumetric golden godrays slicing through the canopy; drifting bioluminescent spore motes.
    - *Layer 4 (Foreground)*: Dirt road littered with roots, decaying foliage, and ambient shadow vignette.
  * Moving the mouse sways the godrays and shifts the tree planes with deep perspective.
* **Exploration Menu**:
  * `[ 🌿 EXPLORE / VENTURE DEEPER ]` (Action Cost: 2 Calories)
  * `[ ⛺ SET BIOMECHANICAL CAMP ]`: Open the Chimera Node-Socket Assembly and Alchemy Crucible.
  * `[ ☀️ SUNLIT GLADE ]`: Photosynthesize (restores calories if floral parts are equipped).
* **Action**:
  * The player clicks `[ 🌿 EXPLORE / VENTURE DEEPER ]`.
  * The camera jolts forward with a rustling branch sound effect.
  * **Event Trigger**: A warning siren pulses in the audio. A wild **Verdant Thorn-Beast** (accompanied by an aggressive Human Poacher) bursts from the underbrush!

### Step 4: The Combat Encounter (Fast Turn-Based Targeting)
* **Visuals**:
  * The forest background darkens with a dramatic depth-of-field blur.
  * The Thorn-Beast and Poacher stand on the right side of the stage.
  * Beneath the beasts, the dirt path is ready to receive blood.
* **Player HUD (Sleek & Uncluttered)**:
  * Shows only your currently active attack limbs and vulnerable parts:
    `[ 🦞 Serrate Claw | Ready ]` • `[ 🗡️ Scavenged Iron Blade | Ready ]` • `[ 🛡️ Chest Plate | 95% ]`
* **Enemy Targeting (Interactive Hover Reticle)**:
  * The player hovers their mouse over the Thorn-Beast's **Left Antler-Stinger**:
    `[ 🎯 Reticle: 82% Hit Chance | Slicing Dmg: 16-22 | Deflection: 2 ]`
  * The player clicks!
  * **Instant Turn Resolution**:
    - The player's Serrate Claw lunges forward with a wet slice sound (`playSlice()`).
    - Combat telemetry logs: `"Your Serrate Claw cleaves into the Thorn-Beast's Antler-Stinger for 20 damage — SEVERED!"`
    - The severed antler drops to the dirt!
    - Arterial bleeding is inflicted; a **dark pool of crimson blood appears and begins expanding** beneath the Thorn-Beast!
  * The Thorn-Beast retaliates with a headbutt, but misses due to disorientation.
  * The player uses their secondary attack limb: a neuro-venom stinger targeting the beast's flank.
  * The beast's **Blood drops below 20%**, and neuro-toxin peaks:
    `"The Verdant Thorn-Beast collapses into hemorrhagic shock! Incapacitated living specimen secured!"`

### Step 5: The Cinematic Post-Combat Surgical Scene
* **Camera Transition**:
  * The battle overlay shatters. The camera tilts up, sweeps across the godrays, and **smoothly zooms directly down onto the wriggling Thorn-Beast** lying in the spreading pool of blood on the mossy soil.
* **Floating Callout HUD (Surgical Triage)**:
  * Floating callout tags fan out radially from the specimen with connecting lines pointing to its anatomy:
    - `[ 🦞 Severed Antler-Stinger (Ground Loot) | 100% Extract | Rare ] ───●`
    - `[ 🫀 Thoracic Bile Sac | 85% Extract | Pulse Cost: -15% | Uncommon ] ───●`
    - `[ 🧠 Ocular Nerve Nexus | 60% Extract | Pulse Cost: -30% | Epic ] ───●`
    - `[ 🩸 Mangled Flank Viscera | [FEAST ONLY: +25 Calories] ] ───●`
  * Top indicator: `SPECIMEN VITAL PULSE: 68% [WEAK]`.
* **Action & Triage**:
  1. The player clicks to harvest the severed antler directly into their sac (free ground loot).
  2. The player clicks to surgically excise the `Thoracic Bile Sac`. Success! Pulse drops to 53%.
  3. The player clicks to extract the delicate `Ocular Nerve Nexus`. Success! The beast flatlines into death right as the eye is removed.
  4. The player clicks `[FEAST ON BLOOD]` to lap up the remaining biomass, restoring calories and earning Biomass EXP.
* **Return to Navigation**:
  * The camera glides back up to the lush canopy view. The player opens their Carrier Sac, now carrying fresh components ready to be grafted into their expanding Chimera node tree!
