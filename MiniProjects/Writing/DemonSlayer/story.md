The smoke from the Infinity Castle’s sudden collapse clears, but instead of the frantic, desperate scramble for survival, an suffocating silence descends upon the ruins.

Tamayo’s hand is still buried deep within Muzan Kibutsuji’s chest. Her face is a mask of fierce, vengeful triumph.

"You're going down with me, Muzan!" she screams, her voice trembling with a century of accumulated rage. "The drug I've injected into you... it converts demons into humans! It's over!"

Muzan doesn't flinch. He doesn't panic. He slowly lowers his head, looking down at the fist embedded in his flesh, and then up at Tamayo.

He furrows his eyebrows.

"Really?" Muzan asks. His voice isn't angry. It is entirely, chillingly flat.

Within his body, a microscopic revolution occurs instantly. The foreign chemical compound surges through his bloodstream, designed to rewrite his genetic makeup, to force his immortal cells to age and wither. But the Demon King’s biology possesses an absolute, conscious will.

Reject.

With a simple, effortless command, his cells completely isolate the drug. They refuse to absorb a single molecule. Instead of bonding with his blood, the liquid is treated as mere debris. Muzan’s flesh parts willingly, creating a hollow, frictionless path right through his internal organs.

Drip. Drip. Drip.

The complex, miraculously engineered humanizing drug simply drains through his torso, spilling uselessly out of his back and splashing onto the shattered tatami mats below.

Tamayo’s eyes widen in absolute horror. "No... No, that's impossible! The composition—"

"You spent decades concocting this?" Muzan asks, genuinely perplexed. He steps forward, effortlessly tearing himself away from her grip as his wound seals instantly. He looks down at the puddle of medicine, then back at her. "I am slightly confused as to why you, of all people, thought something so painfully simple would even have a chance of working on me. Did you truly believe my cellular structure would just... accept a poison because you willed it?"

Before she can scream, a flash of white-hot flesh cleaves through the air. Tamayo is obliterated in a fraction of a second, her remaining ashes scattered into the wind.
The Onslaught

The Demon Slayers arrive like a desperate wave. Gyomei’s spiked flail shatters the air; Sanemi’s wind blades howl with fury; Tanjiro lunges forward, Hinokami Kagura blazing bright against the night sky. They are executing the ultimate ambush, putting their lives on the line to pin down a weakened monster.

But the monster isn’t weakened.

Muzan doesn't run. He doesn't unleash a frantic defense. He just unleashes himself.

Dozens of barbed, whip-like spines erupt from his spine and thighs, moving at speeds that defy human perception. The battlefield becomes a meat grinder.

    The Pillars fall first. Gyomei’s massive frame is pierced through the heart before his flail can even return to his hand. Sanemi is severed at the waist mid-air, his wind breathing dying in a bloody gasp. Obanai and Mitsuri are torn to ribbons in a single, sweeping rotation of Muzan's whips, their hands reaching for each other one last time before hitting the dirt.

    The Next Generation follows. Giyu Tomioka attempts his Eleventh Form, Dead Calm, but the sheer volume and velocity of Muzan's strikes overwhelm the absolute defense, shredding his blade and his torso. Zenitsu’s lightning is extinguished mid-dash; Inosuke’s dual swords are shattered into shrapnel that pierces his own chest.

The Last Ember

Within less than a minute, the entire Corps is erased. The proud, centuries-old organization lies still, reduced to silent corpses in the debris.

Only Tanjiro Kamado remains, brought to his knees, his left arm missing, his breathing ragged and wet. He uses his broken Nichirin blade as a crutch, staring up through a haze of blood at the progenitor of all demons.

Muzan steps over the lifeless body of the Flame Pillar's successor, his pristine white suit completely unstained by the massacre. He stands before Tanjiro, looking down not with hatred, but with a cold, detached pity.

"You changed the breathing styles. You wore the earrings. You fought with such exhausting, tedious sentimentality," Muzan says, his crimson eyes gleaming in the moonlight. "And for what? To die in the dirt like the rest of the insects."

Muzan raises a single hand, his index finger morphing into a sharp, obsidian spike.

"Your breath ends here, Tanjiro Kamado. And with it, the Sun itself goes dark."

With a swift, silent thrust, the spike pierces Tanjiro's forehead. The boy's eyes go wide, the brilliant red of the Hinokami Kagura fading from his pupils, leaving only the cold reflection of a moon that will never see the dawn.

Muzan withdraws his hand, turns his back on the graveyard of the Demon Slayer Corps, and walks out into the night. Nezuko is still out there, completely unprotected. The blue spider lily is no longer needed. The world is finally his.
