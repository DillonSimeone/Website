# DJwish // Neon Street & Live Stage Rig for DJ Deaf Wish
### Dedicated to Nate Hergert (DJ Deaf Wish) — [natehergert.com](https://natehergert.com/)

---

## 1. Concept
One continuous Three.js world, faithful to the archival logo (`assets/images/dj_deaf_wish_logo_original.png`):

- A 460 m smooth-faced gold brick pyramid sits 1.5 km away at the end of a neon city street,
  rotated 45 degrees so two faces read like the logo (left face lit, right face in shade).
- A violet laser sheet fans from the apex up into a sky-scale (~985 m) slanted "DJ DEAF WISH"
  whose baseline sits exactly on the fan's far edge, like the artwork.
- The visitor lands in a rain-slick street canyon. The bio and technical rider are spray-painted
  murals on the walls; the audio station and live-rig links are real HTML paste-ups on plywood
  street hoardings. Scrolling walks the camera backward down the street, pyramid always looming.

Scale is the point: 1 unit = 1 m, camera eye height ~2 m, streetlights / signs / windows give
the size reference.

```
street mouth (z=+280) -> neon canyon (to z=-170) -> plaza with processional lights -> pyramid (z=-1500)
```

## 2. Experience Flow
1. **Intro (14 s, skip with SPACE / click / wheel / scroll)** - Catmull-Rom spline flight:
   foot of the pyramid looking up the brick face -> tilt and climb to the apex while the laser
   sheet ignites outward -> the sky logo -> long ~1.3 km pull-back, descending into the street.
2. **Scroll rig (5 stops, 600vh scroll track)**
   | Stop | Content | Where |
   |---|---|---|
   | 0 `#street` | Title mural + pyramid hero shot | right facade near the street end |
   | 1 `#agency` | "Deaf Sonic Agency" bio mural | left firewall behind a 60 m low-rise gap |
   | 2 `#rider` | "Technical Rider" mural | right firewall behind a 60 m low-rise gap |
   | 3 `#audio` | Live audio station (HTML) | left sidewalk hoarding |
   | 4 `#links` | Launch live rig, natehergert.com, haptics test | right sidewalk hoarding |
   Section dots, arrow / PageUp / PageDown keys and URL hashes jump between stops. Narrow screens
   turn the camera toward the content (`focusYaw`), wide screens frame content + pyramid.
3. **Live Stage mode (SPACE)** - the sky logo fades and the pyramid draws the visuals itself
   (`sky-show.js`, all GPU, fed by the audio texture):
   - [1] SKY SPECTRUM: 48 laser-outlined spectrum bars in the sky, each wired to the apex by a beam.
   - [2] SKY SCOPE: triggered oscilloscope ribbon in the sky with beams from the apex.
   - [3] HYPER TUNNEL: laser rings stream out of the apex and down the street (floor + walls);
     the camera pulls all the way back above the street mouth.
   - [4] STREET VU: the streetlights are a volume meter. Silence = all off; they light one by one
     from the pyramid end toward the camera as the volume rises (red near the pyramid, rotating to
     purple at the street mouth), with a white peak-hold lamp. The pyramid fills up from its base
     in step with them.
   - [5] SEARCHLIGHTS: six band-coloured searchlights sweep from the capstone; each pyramid face
     pulses its own band and the edges strobe on the beat.
   Each scene also drives a brick-face animation on the pyramid (pyramid.js `PYRAMID_FX`):
   band courses (1), beat rings climbing the faces (2), pulses pouring from the apex (3),
   VU fill (4), face split (5). Scenes are configured in `STAGE_SCENES` (world.js).
   Keystoning [C], MIDI [M], haptics [V], fullscreen [F].
4. **Settings disc ([S], SETTINGS button, gear in the live rig bar)** - the camera flies to the
   capstone, which flares, and a vinyl record rises spinning out of it on a light beam and settles on
   section A. Six sections sit 60 degrees apart on the record (A AUDIO, B COLOUR, C LIGHT,
   D PYRAMID, E CITY, F CAMERA). Clicking another section, the hub arrows or Left/Right spins the
   record until that section is level at the top. Values save to localStorage
   (`djwish.settings.v1`); RESET ALL (click twice) restores the defaults. ESC / CLOSE sinks the
   disc back into the capstone and returns to the previous street stop or live rig scene.
   The schema (labels, ranges, defaults) is `SETTINGS_SCHEMA` in `src/settings/settings-store.js`;
   `applySetting` in app.js maps each key onto the engine.

## 2b. Sound-Reactive Colour Convention
Low frequencies are red; the colour follows frequency around the wheel up to purple at the top
(`hueColor` / `bandColor` in `AUDIO_PARS`, `HUE_MAX = 0.78`). `uHue` is the energy-weighted band
position, `uBands[7]` are adaptively normalised 0..1 band levels, `uActive` fades everything back
to the idle brand palette when the audio stops. Reactive surfaces: laser fan (live spectrum),
capstone + apex glow, pyramid edges, logo halo, street VU-meter buildings, equaliser neon strips,
shop fronts, street reflections, mural paint, signs.

To move things, edit `world.js` only: murals, hoardings, camera stops and the intro path all read
from it, and the city reserves building footprints (`RESERVED`) for the mural walls.

## 4. Performance Budget (target 60 fps mid GPU, 120 where available)
- ~25 draw calls, 2 lights (moon directional + hemisphere). Buildings, skyline, lamps, glows and
  sparkles are each a single InstancedMesh; signs and neon strips are merged geometry.
- All audio reactivity runs through shared shader uniforms (`SHARED` in world.js), updated once per
  frame. No per-frame geometry rebuilds, no per-frame allocations.
- Textures (bricks, murals, signs, logo) are painted once at boot; no shadowBlur anywhere.
- QualityManager tiers: dpr 1.5 + bloom -> 1.25 -> 1.0 -> no bloom -> 0.85 -> 0.75, stepping on a
  90-frame rolling average. Bloom (UnrealBloomPass, threshold 1.0 so only HDR neon/lasers bloom)
  plus a grain/vignette pass run only on the high tiers. Touch devices start without bloom.
- Render loop pauses on `visibilitychange`. CSS3D only renders while a panel is visible.
- Append `?fps` to the URL for an on-screen FPS / tier readout.

## 5. Accessibility
The mural text is baked into textures, so `index.html` carries an sr-only copy of the title,
bio and rider. Keep it in sync with `src/data/artist-profile.js`.
