About

Maximize Your Tech, LLC

A Deaf-owned shop in Philomath. Live captions and broadcast for events. Prints, engraving, and marked goods. Computers built from a published list.

Mayan Fogarty is the owner, a certified systems administrator, and has run live captioning for events around Portland plus online broadcast support. Susan handles client accounts. She has a BA in business management and a background in sales. Both are hard of hearing.

The company works with the Deaf community and has volunteered with CymaSpace, a nonprofit whose mission is arts access for Deaf and hard of hearing people.
