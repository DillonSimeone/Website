Our Mission

Both my partner and I are hard of hearing individuals who have a strong tie to the deaf community. We understand the needs of this community and are actively working to promote equal accessibility. Susan has a BA in business management working in sales and I have been certified as a Systems Administrator. Both of us have been working and volunteering at CymaSpace, a nonprofit organization whose mission is to make the Arts accessible to the Deaf and Hard of Hearing. I have been commissioned to set up auto captioning at various events around Portland and work online with OBS broadcasting support. If you wish to make your venue, or business, accessible to the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community Susan and I possess the skills and experience to help you meet your objectives.
