The old site could not take an order.

It asked people to shop, then told a personal story, then put another organization’s cafe calendar in the same menu as custom PCs. A venue, a shop, and someone buying a computer all landed on one page and could not see a price, a photo, or a button that went anywhere.

Open the before beside the rebuild. The wording in the before is theirs, including the typos. This page is the list of what had to change, and what the rebuild refused to invent.

Captions on the room screens. Streams that stay up. Goods with your mark on them.

Voice that had to go

On the old site

Both my partner and I are hard of hearing individuals who have a strong tie to the deaf community.
l do online OBS streaming.
Susan and I also offer 3D printing as well.
I’m launching this business to help support the community around me.
Overlooking this community means missing out of a pool of dedicated, knowledgeable individuals.

Deaf-owned shop in Philomath. Event access, fabrication, and computers for Portland and the valley.
Mayan Fogarty is the owner, a certified systems administrator.
Susan handles client accounts. She has a BA in business management and a background in sales.
One email. The Gmail address is not published.
